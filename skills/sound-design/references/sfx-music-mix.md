# Sound (MMH video essay)

Follow `bible-sfx.md` (three layers: atmosphere, movement, emphasis) and the style bible's Sound section; in the Faceless Berribot project also `claude/sfx-library.md`.
- **Gridd AI reels:** **one popular track** (well-known artists that are popular on TikTok/short-form; fine for Instagram) under the whole video, with no sudden music changes. Offer 3–8 options mixed on the same picture when the user hasn't picked (the user rejected Desert Rose as "news report bg" and picked Hanumankind's Big Dawgs for Fevicol). Check the song for its own silences or breakdowns before choosing `srcStart` (the Big Dawgs instrumental stops at 48.6–50.7 s; start at 50.7). Choose the start offset so more cuts than chance land within 70 ms of a beat.
- **Berribot / replicas:** music follows the story world, section by section — one bed under everything sounds wrong there. When replicating, map the reference's own bed first (earshot timeline on the separated `NN_bed.wav`) and mirror its arc. MMH's PayPal reel: lo-fi hip-hop with pizzicato (mob setup) → music box (Goodfellas quote) → lo-fi (fraud story) → house with deep sub (Igor build) → hit (Scarface) → aggressive lo-fi with sub (payoff).
- **Approved beds:** Lo-Fi 04 (start 9 s), New Bass 02 (start 8.23 s; later downbeat 30.8 s), Deep Urban (start 23.22 s), plus the tracks in the approved PayPal v3 mix (user picked mix B, Oct 30): "Luxurious Layer – Golden Era Boom Bap Flow" by Rockot (start 0.7 s, confident setup), "Lofi Song – Toybox" by Lofium (start 0.1 s, playful music-box piano for a quote beat), "Jazz Lo Fi Beat" by YuraSoop (start 0.04 s, calm explanation), Deep Urban (build), New Bass 02 (30.8 s, payoff). Popular instrumentals already downloaded are in `_Global library/music/songs (popular, instrumental)`. Taste: confident, driving, plucked-synth electronic, boom-bap or the popular track. Never mysterious, tense, sad, cinematic, horror or trap beds.
- **New royalty-free tracks** need approval with 2–3 test mixes on the same picture. Sources: Mixkit, or Pixabay (Pixabay Content License) taking only tracks with `isAiGenerated: false` and `hasYoutubeContentId: false`. Pixabay search from a pixabay.com tab: fetch `/music/search/<query>/`, read `__BOOTSTRAP_URL__` from the HTML, fetch that JSON, use `page.results[]` (`name`, `duration`, `sources.src` mp3, `user.username`, the two flags). Rank against reference windows with CLAP (earshot `vocab.embed_files` on 8 s windows) only as a first filter, then read each finalist's `earshot timeline` sections and moods.
- For section-based mixes, switch sections on the story turns, as a hard switch on a cut, starting the new track on a downbeat (`earshot timeline --out x.json` → `music.downbeats`). If one track spans a voiced part and a voiceless beat, split it into two segments with a continuous `srcStart` so its level can change on the cut.
- **SFX, Gridd AI reels (the user asked for MMH-style ticks):** a tick on every caption word (key press/tick −14 to −18 dB; a button click on big words, −9 dB), key presses while UI text types in, a camera shutter when a photo or print lands, a paper slide for prints, a soft click on highlights, a whoosh peaking on each whip/slide cut and on fast pans, a marker stroke on circles, clicks as the end-card logo builds, a short glitch SFX on every InvertGlitch burst (trimmed to the burst, −10 to −13 dB), an electric glitch on every ShapeGlitch burst (−15 dB), at most one impact (CTA or reveal). Target 20–35 events per minute; no two whooshes within 1.5 s. Files are in `_Global library/sfx/normalized` (transient at t=0; place at the frame).
- **SFX, Berribot projects:** light only, from the approved list: pops, paper slides, soft clicks, small sweeps. Never sub drops, risers, impacts, drones, glitches or heartbeats. About 1 cut in 3–4 gets one, with its peak on the cut frame (place at `frame - peak_offset`).
- **Mix:** measure the VO first (Voice Studio VO ≈ −20 LUFS; about −18.4 after the clarity pass). **Words must stay clear above everything.** In talky sections the bed ends up ~10–12 dB under the voice after ducking (@artzenmedia's 11.5M-view reel: VO −19.7, bed −31.5). Earlier rule for section-based Berribot mixes: 7–8 dB below (≈ −27/−28 LUFS for the opening, −26.5/−27.5 later), each measured over its exact span with ffmpeg ebur128; the −23.5 LUFS bed (only ~3 dB under) was too loud.
  - Gridd mixes (cold brew v2): bed at VO −2.5 LU with a sidechain duck of **10.5 dB** from the VO (10 ms / 250 ms); 6.5–8.5 dB let the music cloud words. Optionally −3 dB at 2.5 kHz on the music. +3 dB in voiceless beats and on the end card.
  - Small SFX (key presses, ticks, whooshes) go on a bus that dips 5 dB while the voice talks; reveals and glitches stay at full level.
  - Voiceless payoffs come up to about −21/−22 LUFS or louder. Fade the first bed in over ~12 f (8 ms when the first beat lands on frame 0).
  - Check intelligibility: run ASR on the master against the script; every word should align, and compare per-word confidence with the dry VO.
- Mix offline: a small numpy mixer that reads the same `sound.json` as the composition's `SoundTrack` (VO at its offset, music segments with `srcStart`/fades/volume/duck/env, SFX at frames) writes the WAV in ~2 s and matches Remotion's output to 0.1 LU; `remotion render --codec=wav` takes 3–4 min. Master two-pass loudnorm to −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP, then mux onto the silent picture render (`-c:v copy`). Audio-only changes never need a picture re-render.
